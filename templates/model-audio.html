MODULE 02

Audio & Speech Analysis

Acoustic feature extraction and ensemble learning for detecting Alzheimer's vocal biomarkers.

Feature Engineering

We extracted 19 distinct acoustic features using librosa:

  • 13 Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCCs) for vocal tract modeling.
  • Pitch and RMS Energy for speech dynamics.
  • Pause ratio (silence detection) for hesitation markers.
  • Zero Crossing Rate (ZCR) and Spectral Centroid.

Data Augmentation

To combat the small dataset size, we added low-amplitude white noise to the original 318 samples, effectively doubling the training set to over 600 samples and improving model robustness.

Benchmarking

Using Stratified K-Fold Cross-Validation (5 folds), we evaluated three algorithms:

Algorithm F1-Score (CV)
Logistic Regression 0.7045
Random Forest 0.7587
SVM (Optimized) 0.7971
Voting Classifier (Winner) 0.8016
Why the Voting Classifier?

Instead of relying on a single model, the Voting Classifier combines the probabilities of Random Forest, SVM, and Logistic Regression. This ensemble approach reduced variance and achieved the highest generalization capability (F1-Score > 0.80).
