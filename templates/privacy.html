LEGAL & ETHICS

Privacy Policy

Compliance with Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 (Habeas Data).

1. Data Collection and Purpose

NeuroFusion AI processes medical images (MRI) and audio recordings for the sole purpose of academic research and cognitive screening evaluation. This is a proof-of-concept prototype.

2. Anonymization

The system is designed to process data anonymously. We do not store real patient names or sensitive personal identifiers in our database. Predictions are linked to sequential IDs (e.g., user_id=1) or anonymous placeholders.

3. Data Security

Data is processed over secure connections (HTTPS). Passwords for medical staff accounts are cryptographically hashed (using Werkzeug security), ensuring they cannot be reversed or read by developers.

4. User Rights

As per Law 1581, users have the right to know, update, and request the deletion of their personal data. Since this is an academic prototype, no real clinical data should be uploaded to the production environment on Render.

Disclaimer: NeuroFusion AI is an educational prototype. It does not provide medical diagnoses. Always consult a certified neurologist or healthcare professional for clinical evaluation.
