MODULE 03

Late Fusion (Ensemble)

Combining structural and functional biomarkers via meta-classification.

The Fusion Strategy

Because the MRI and Audio datasets come from different patients, we could not perform a direct 1-to-1 merge. Instead, we implemented Synthetic Late Fusion.

We randomly sampled 3,000 combinations of MRI probabilities and Audio probabilities. The final ground truth label was determined by a Clinical Severity Rule: y_true = max(mri_label, audio_label).

Input Features

The meta-classifier receives a 6-dimensional vector:

  • prob_normal_mri, prob_mild_mri, prob_moderate_mri
  • prob_normal_audio, prob_mild_audio, prob_moderate_audio

Benchmarking

We trained three meta-classifiers on the synthetic dataset:

Meta-Classifier F1-Score
SVM 0.7400
Random Forest 0.7800
Logistic Regression (Winner) 0.8000
Why Logistic Regression?

Logistic Regression is inherently probabilistic. It acts as a perfect mathematical arbitrator, learning exactly how much weight to give to the MRI versus the Audio prediction when making the final decision.
